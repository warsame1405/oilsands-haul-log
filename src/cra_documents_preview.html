🤖 What "Auto-Filled" means

TruckPilot already has your gross revenue (loads), expenses (receipts + categories), fuel log (litres + cost), and driver pay (payroll). All of those numbers map directly to CRA form fields — no manual entry.

✅ What's 100% Automatic

GST collected = 5% of all load earnings. ITCs = sum of GST on each expense category (fuel, repairs, parts, tools). Net = collected minus credits. All from your existing logged data — zero data entry.

⚠️ What User Must Confirm

Business registration number (BN), GST number, reporting period (quarterly vs annual), and whether any revenue is GST-exempt. App prompts once during setup and remembers.

✅ Auto-Filled Lines on T2125

Revenue (8000), Fuel (8811), Insurance (8690), Repairs (8960), Licenses (8760), Subcontract/driver pay. These map 1-to-1 from your TruckPilot expense categories to CRA line numbers.

⚠️ User Must Confirm

CCA Class 10 (truck value, purchase year, UCC balance). Business use % of vehicle if mixed personal/business. Business Number (BN). These are one-time setup fields the app saves for future years.

📐 Implementation — What's Needed
