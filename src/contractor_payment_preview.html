📋 How this feature works
1️⃣
Load has invoice sent
You complete a load, tap "Invoice" — the load gets an invoice number and status "Awaiting Payment".
2️⃣
Contractor pays you
You receive the money (EFT, cheque, cash). Tap "Mark as Paid" on the load — enter date, confirm amount, payment method.
3️⃣
History grouped by period
All confirmed payments appear in Report → Received Payments, grouped by your pay period preference (weekly / bi-weekly / monthly).
⚙️ Period preference
The grouping (weekly / bi-weekly / monthly) follows the same pay period setting already in your Rates & Settings. No new setting needed — it reuses what you already have. So if you pay drivers bi-weekly, your contractor receipts are also grouped bi-weekly.
📌 Where it lives in the app
Mark as Paid button → on every load card (Loads tab) and inside the Load Detail modal.
Payment History → Report tab → new "💼 Received Payments" section below the existing Summary cards.
Outstanding invoices → shown in red/amber inside the same section so you always see what's still owed.
