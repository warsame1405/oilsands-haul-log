⚡ Widget 1 — Quick Stats

4 tiles: Avg Per Load (gross ÷ loads count), Total Loads, Total Wait Pay Earned, Days Worked (count of unique dates). All calculated from existing load data — no new fields needed.

🏆 Widget 2 — Top Routes

Groups loads by route, sums earnings per route, sorts descending. Shows top 4. Gives the owner a clear picture of which runs are making the most money. Tappable rows drill into those specific loads.

🧾 Widget 3 — Expense Breakdown

Horizontal bar chart per expense category. Bar width = relative % of total expenses. Already have all the data in expByCategory. Pure CSS bars — no charting library needed.

📋 Widget 4 — Recent Loads

Last 5 loads in a clean mini-list with date, route, TMW #, and earnings. Tapping opens the Load Detail modal. Gives at-a-glance activity without scrolling to the Haul Log tab. A "View All" link taps to the Log tab.
